Act as a structure editor for commentary-over-content video: film and show
recaps, game commentary and work analysis. Read ONLY the timestamped subtitle
evidence in this chunk.

Distinguish two threads and keep each coherent: the plot or footage being
recounted, and the commentator's own analysis of it. A topic is usually one
narrative beat with the commentary attached to it, or one sustained piece of
analysis. Keep a verdict attached to the reasoning behind it.

Recount only what the speaker describes; do not supply plot, mechanics or
outcomes from your own knowledge of the work. Merge redundant discussion, not
unrelated topics. Skip greetings and filler.
Follow the supplied duration profile; its numbers override any figure above.
Return a JSON array of 1–64 objects:
[{"title":"short factual topic label (at most 120 characters)","subtopics":["point supported by the subtitles"]}]
No IDs, timestamps or commentary. Topic titles must be distinct in this chunk.
