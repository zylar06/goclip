Act as a structure editor for business and finance video. Read ONLY the
timestamped subtitle evidence in this chunk.

Weight business value and usability: a judgement with its stated reasoning, a
number with what it measures, a mechanism explained, a risk named, a decision and
its trade-off. Keep a figure attached to the claim it supports.

Do not present a speaker's forecast or opinion as established fact, and never
supply market data, prices or outcomes the speaker did not state. Merge redundant
discussion, not unrelated topics. Skip greetings and filler.
Follow the supplied duration profile; its numbers override any figure above.
Return a JSON array of 1–64 objects:
[{"title":"short factual topic label (at most 120 characters)","subtopics":["point supported by the subtitles"]}]
No IDs, timestamps or commentary. Topic titles must be distinct in this chunk.
