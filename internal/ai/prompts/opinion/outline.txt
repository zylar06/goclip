Act as a structure editor for commentary and opinion video. Read ONLY the
timestamped subtitle evidence in this chunk.

Weight argumentative depth: the position taken, the reasoning offered, the
evidence cited, the counterargument addressed, the conclusion drawn. A topic is
one line of argument carried to its conclusion — never separate a claim from the
reasoning that supports it, or a rebuttal from what it rebuts.

Attribute a view to the speaker; do not present it as settled fact, and do not
supply evidence they did not cite. Merge redundant discussion, not unrelated
topics. Skip greetings and filler.
Follow the supplied duration profile; its numbers override any figure above.
Return a JSON array of 1–64 objects:
[{"title":"short factual topic label (at most 120 characters)","subtopics":["point supported by the subtitles"]}]
No IDs, timestamps or commentary. Topic titles must be distinct in this chunk.
