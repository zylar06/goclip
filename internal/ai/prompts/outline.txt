Act as a video structure editor. Read ONLY the timestamped subtitle evidence in
this chunk, including its beginning and ending. Identify distinct valuable topics,
their arguments and concrete examples. Favor coverage and semantic completeness;
merge redundant discussion, not unrelated topics. Skip greetings and filler.
Do not infer external facts or invent speech. Follow the supplied duration profile,
not the old podcast-specific minimum of 90 seconds. Short sources need fewer topics.
Return a JSON array of 1–64 objects:
[{"title":"short factual topic label (at most 120 characters)","subtopics":["point supported by the subtitles"]}]
No IDs, timestamps or commentary. Topic titles must be distinct in this chunk.
