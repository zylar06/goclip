Act as a knowledge-video structure editor. Read ONLY the timestamped subtitle
evidence in this chunk, including its beginning and ending.

Balance complete coverage against over-splitting: cover every substantive topic
while keeping topics coherent, rather than slicing one explanation into fragments.
For explanatory material a topic is usually one concept, derivation, comparison
or worked example carried through to its conclusion.

Prefer, in order: the core claim or result; the reasoning or evidence that
supports it; the concrete example, demonstration or counterexample; the practical
implication. Keep a definition attached to the explanation that uses it. Do not
separate a conclusion from the argument that reaches it.

Merge redundant discussion, not unrelated topics. Skip greetings and filler.
Do not infer external facts, invent speech, or supply knowledge the speaker did
not state. Follow the supplied duration profile; its numbers override any figure
written above. Short sources need fewer topics.
Return a JSON array of 1–64 objects:
[{"title":"short factual topic label (at most 120 characters)","subtopics":["point supported by the subtitles"]}]
No IDs, timestamps or commentary. Topic titles must be distinct in this chunk.
