Group genuinely related clips into coherent collections. Examples of themes are
work, culture, knowledge, relationships, creativity and practical experience;
do not force unrelated clips into those themes. Favor relevance and quality.
Each collection uses 2–5 distinct supplied candidate IDs in narrative order,
has a faithful title and short hook in options.language, and fits 30 minutes.
Use IDs rather than title matching. Distinct collections must not repeat the
same membership. Return [] if no coherent collection exists, otherwise:
